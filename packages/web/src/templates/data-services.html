Frequently asked questions

What is managed data extraction (or managed web scraping)?
A fully managed service where Zyte builds, runs, and maintains your web data pipeline end to end — you define the data you need, we deliver it clean and structured.
When should I outsource web data collection instead of building in-house?
When maintenance, anti-bot handling, and compliance would pull your engineers away from core product work.
How does Zyte Data (managed data services) work?
We scope your project, deliver sample data fast, then launch and maintain production feeds with a dedicated team.
What types of web data projects can Zyte handle?
From e-commerce and real estate to news, search, social, and training data for AI — at any scale.
How long does it take to launch a managed data project?
Our structured onboarding prioritizes speed-to-value, with sample data early and production feeds shortly after.
How is data quality ensured in a managed service?
QA is built into the lifecycle, with validation, monitoring, and human oversight before delivery.
Is outsourcing web scraping more cost-effective than building in-house?
Often yes — you avoid infrastructure, maintenance, and the hidden cost of failed runs and engineer time.
How does Zyte handle site blocking and anti-bot systems?
Powered by Zyte API, the proven leader in overcoming site blocks.
Can Zyte deliver data in our preferred format and schedule?
Yes — CSV, JSON, or XML, pushed to GCS, S3, Azure, AWS, or your tools on your schedule.
Is Zyte Data managed web data extraction compliant and secure?
Each project is reviewed for regulatory risk with legal collaboration to support GDPR, CCPA, and EU AI Act alignment.
