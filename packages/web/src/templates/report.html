Section 01

Executive summary

Web data collection continues to shift from hand-maintained scrapers toward AI-assisted extraction. This report analyses extraction reliability, cost and compliance across a representative sample of commercial websites.

Key finding. AI-assisted extraction reduced selector-maintenance effort by 71% while holding field accuracy above 96% across the sample.

The remainder of this document details the methodology, the benchmark results, and practical recommendations for teams operating at scale.
